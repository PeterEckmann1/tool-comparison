Methods ESCC tissue specimens Forty pairs of tumor tissues and adjacent normal esophageal squamous tissues were obtained from ESCC patients who underwent esophagogastrostomy in The Fourth Hospital of Hebei Medical University from January 2015 to December 2019. The study participants had an average age of 61.6±8.0 (range, 47–73) years; 25 patients were male and 15 were female. None of the patients were administered chemotherapy or radiotherapy preoperatively. Clinicopathological features of the patients were collected for the analysis. The ESCC tissues and adjacent normal tissues were all stored in liquid nitrogen. The Ethical Review Committee of the Fourth Hospital of Hebei Medical University granted approval for this study. Each patient signed an informed consent form. The study was carried out in line with the Declaration of Helsinki (as revised in 2013). Eca-109 cell culture Human EC cell line Eca-109 was supplied by American Type Culture Collection (USA). Culture of Eca-109 cells in Roswell Park Memorial Institute-1640 (RPMI-1640) supplemented with 10% (v/v) fetal bovine serum (FBS) (Gibco, Thermo), penicillin (80 unit/mL) and streptomycin (100 µg/mL) (Life Technologies, Inc.) was conducted, and the cell culture was maintained at 37 °C with 5% CO2. Transfection with miR-449a-5p mimic or miR-449a-5p inhibitor Negative control mimic (NC), miR-449a-5p mimic (449aM), negative control inhibitor (NCI), and miR-449a-5p inhibitor (449aI) were obtained from Genepharm (China). HiPerfect Transfection Reagent (Qiagen) was used to transfect miRNA oligos into Eca-109 cells following the manufacturer’s protocol. In short, the day prior to transfection, 1.0×105 cells were seeded in a 6-well-plate. All miRNA oligos were diluted in 150 µL double-distilled water (ddH2O) to 20 µm solution. Then, 2 µL miRNA oligos solution (20 µm) and 3 µL Hiperfect transfection reagent were added to 100 µL RPMI-1640. After incubation at room temperature for 10 min, the mixture was added to each well. The cells were cultured further at 37 °C in an atmosphere of 5% CO2 for 48 h. Sequences of the miRNA oligos are described in Table 1 . RNA samples extracted from cells and tissues were diluted with diethyl pyrocarbonate-treated water (DEPC H2O) into a 1 µg/µL RNA solution. First, 1 µL RNA was mixed with 1 µL microRNA-specific strand-loop reverse transcription (RT)-primer (500 nm), 2 µL dNTP (10 mM), and 6 µL DEPC H2O. Next, the mixture was heated at 70 °C for 10 min, and then cooled to 4 °C. Following that, 0.5 µL MMLV Reverse Transcriptase (200 U/µL), 2 µL 10× Transcriptase Buffer, 0.5 µL Recombinant RNase inhibitor (40 U/µL), and 7 µL DEPC H2O were added into the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) tube; the mixture was heated at 42 °C for 60 min, 95 °C for 5 min, and cooled to 4 °C. TargetScan (http://www.targetscan.org/), miRanda (http://www.targetscan.org/), and PicTar (http://pictar.mdc-berlin.de/) were employed for the prediction of miR-449a-5p’s target gene. B-cell lymphoma 2 (Bcl-2) was identified as a predicted target gene of miR-449a-5p. A predicted binding site of miR-449a-5p was located on the 3’-UTR of Bcl-2 messenger RNA (mRNA). DNA fragments of the 3’-UTR of Bcl-2 mRNA were cloned by PCR. The primer sequences used for PCR are described in Table 4 (restriction sites are underlined). Genomic DNA (1 µg) was used as the PCR template. We created a mixture with 1 µg sample of genomic DNA (1 µg/µL), 0.8 µL primers (10 µm), 10 µL polymerase mix, and 8.2 µL ddH2O. The PCR conditions were as follows: 95 °C for 10 min, followed by 40 cycles for 95 °C for 15 s, 55 °C for 45 s, 72 °C for 1 min, then 72 °C for 2 min, and cooled to 4 °C. The PCR product was purified using a TaKaRa MiniBEST Agarose Gel Extraction Kit, and then endonuclease EcoRI and XbaI were used for digestion of the purified PCR. Finally, the DNA fragment was inserted into the pmiRGLO vector (Promega) with T4 ligase (Promega) to construct the recombinant luciferase vector (pmiRGLO-3’-UTR). For the luciferase assay, Eca-109 cells underwent transfection with the recombinant luciferase vector, control vector, NC, miR-449a-5p mimic, NCI and miR-449a-5p inhibitor using Effectene Transfection Reagent (Qiagen) following the manufacturer’s instructions. One day before transfection, 5,000 Eca-109 cells were seeded in a 96-well plate and subsequently subjected to culture in 100 µL RPMI-1640 supplemented with 10% (v/v) FBS (Gibco), penicillin (80 unit/mL), and streptomycin (100 µg/mL). Next, 3.75 µL miRNA mimic or inhibitor (20 µm), 2 g vector or recombinant vector, 116.25 µL EC buffer, and 8 µL enhancer buffer were added to 100 µL medium. After mixing, the solution was incubated for 10 min at room temperature. Luciferase activity was detected with a dual-luciferase reporter assay system (Promega), with normalization to Renilla luciferase activity to control the transfection efficiency. All tests were performed in triplicate. MTT assay Cell proliferation was measured by MTT assay. Briefly, Eca-109 cells (1×104) were seeded in a 96-well plate and subjected to culture for 24, 48, or 72 h. Then, the addition of 20 µL MTT (10 mg/mL) was made to each well, and the cells were incubated at 37 °C with 5% CO2 for 4 h. After the removal of the supernatant, 150 µL dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) was added to the cells, which were then incubated at room temperature for 15–20 min to dissolve the purple formazan crystals. An enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) instrument was used to measure absorbance at 450 nm. Each test was carried out six times. Colony formation assay One day after transfection with microRNA oligos, a 6-well plate was seeded with Eca-109 cells (300 cells per well) and maintained for 14 days at 37 °C in an atmosphere containing 5% CO2. The medium containing miRNA mimics or inhibitors transfection mixture was refreshed once a week. After washing three times with phosphate-buffered saline (PBS), the cells were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) at room temperature for 20 min. Next, the cells were again washed three times with PBS, and dyed with 2.5% crystal violet staining solution (diluted in methanol) at room temperature for 30 min. Then, after five more washes with PBS, the cells were dried at room temperature. The number of colonies in each well (containing >50 cells) was calculated with the bare eye. The relative colony number was determined as the ratio of the number of colonies formed by cells transfected with microRNA mimic or inhibitor to the number of colonies formed by cells transfected with negative control mimic or inhibitor. All tests were repeated three times. Western blot After 48 h of transfection with microRNA oligos, Eca-109 cells were harvested. Total protein was extracted using RIPA with protease inhibitor cocktail and phosphatase inhibitor cocktail. In line with the accompanying protocol, a BCA kit was used to determine the protein concentration of the samples. Samples of protein (15 µL) extracted from the Eca-109 cells were subjected to 10% sodium dodecyl sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE) separation followed by transfer to a polyvinylidene difluoride (PVDF) membrane (Millipore). Then, membrane blocking was carried out at room temperature for 2 h using 5% nonfat milk (diluted in tris-buffered saline with 0.1% Tween-20, TBST). The membrane was incubated with primary antibodies for Bcl-2, Bcl-2-associated X protein (BAX), and glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate dehydrogenase (GAPDH; 1:1,000 diluted in TBST). Then, the membrane was washed 5 times with TBST (10 min/time) and incubated with horseradish peroxidase (HRP)-labeled secondary anti-rabbit IgG (1:1,000 diluted in TBST) at room temperature for 2 h. After washing the membrane 5 times (10 min/time) with TBST, we detected the protein bands using an enhanced chemiluminescence (ECL) kit in accordance with the protocol supplied with the kit. The relative protein expressions were determined with GAPDH used for normalization. All tests were repeated three times. Statistical analysis The SPSS 13.0 statistical software package was used to perform the data analyses in this study. All data were expressed as mean ± standard error of the mean (SEM). Differences between two groups were analyzed using the non-parametric Mann–Whitney U test. Statistically significant differences between four groups were determined through one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA). P<0.05 represented a statistically significant difference.
