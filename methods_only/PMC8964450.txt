Materials and Methods K3[Fe(CN)6]·3H2O, PVP and hydrochloric acid were purchased from Sigma–Aldrich. Chlorin e6 (Ce6) was obtained from Cayman. Antibodies for tissue slices were obtained from Abclonal. The HypoxyprobeTM-1 Plus Kit was purchased from Hypoxyprobe, Inc. Morphology was characterized by a Tecnai G2 spirit BioTwin transmission electron microscope. The hydrodynamic diameters and zeta potentials were characterized by a Zetasizer Nano ZS90. Photoacoustic images were collected from MSOT inSight/inVision 256. The oxygen content in the O2 generation curve was detected by portable dissolved oxygen meters (JPBJ-608, Shanghai REX Instrument Factory).
