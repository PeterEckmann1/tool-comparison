Method details Chronic venous cannulation has already been established in mice in the 1960s and was first described for the jugular vein in 1979 [1,2]. Over the years these publications were followed by various studies refining the previously described techniques [3,4]. We extensively studied the literature, evaluated the (dis-) advantages of each proposed technique and then combined this method with the implantation of a recording electrode array. This novel approach allows the investigation of a broad field of complex neurophysiological processes.
