Materials and Methods This retrospective case series included three patients with significant smoking history who were treated with subcutaneous TCZ (SC-TCZ) for active TED refractory to orbital radiation and systemic steroids. Unlike prior studies, variable TCZ dosing strategies were used. Patients were followed between 14 and 27 months. Data including pre- and posttreatment CAS, proptosis reduction, and self-reported subjective change in quality of life were extracted from patient charts. The study was performed in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and adhered to the ethical principles for medical research involving human subjects outlined in the Declaration of Helsinki of 2013. Institutional Review Board approval was waived owing to the small study size.
